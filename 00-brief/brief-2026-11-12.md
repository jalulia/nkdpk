## The assignment

Create the US consumer brand name, visual identity, and master outer-box packaging for NakedPak’s DTC soup and stew launch (late March). “NakedPak” is the B2B seaweed-film name; “Deserved” was an investor placeholder.

Scope (Nov 13 to Dec 18 sprint): 3 naming territories, visual identity + guidelines, and master outer box for 1 Hero SKU (6 dissolvable pouches per box, built on FPO dimensions and panels).

## The opportunity

**Culinary comfort without plastic or prep.** Chef-crafted bone-broth and plant-based soups where the seaweed pouch dissolves in boiling water in two minutes, thickening the broth with zero waste and zero plastic contact.

**A whole new cooking ritual.** Proving consumers love the “drop, cook, dissolve” ritual in DTC creates a category of one and unlocks B2B food partners.

## The audience

**Primary (Busy Households & Working Professionals):** Wants a nourishing, cozy meal in minutes without sacrificing flavor, clean ingredients, or easy prep.

**Secondary (Outdoor & On-the-Go):** Campers, travelers, and students who want lightweight, packable real meals with zero trash left behind.

## The strategic tension

People want effortless, waste-free comfort food, but “eating the wrapper” sounds like a science experiment. Lead with tech and shoppers hesitate; hide the pouch and they miss the magic of the one-step ritual.

## The strategic lens

Food first, magic second. Sell the steaming bowl and chef-crafted broth before explaining the film. Frame the seaweed pouch as natural as an apple or tomato skin: part of the recipe that locks in flavor and melts into the broth.

## The desired response

The work should make people feel: “That looks delicious. Wait, I just drop the whole pouch in boiling water and it melts into the broth? I want to try that.”

Craving before curiosity. Warm, culinary, effortless, and naturally smart, never clinical or gimmicky.

## Initial creative territory

**Naming:** Lead with culinary warmth, nourishment, or the effortless ritual rather than packaging tech. Warm, appetizing, and extensible beyond soup (ramen, oats, sauces).

**3-Second Box Hierarchy:** (1) Appetizing food-first brand and flavor call, (2) foolproof “drop, cook, dissolve” ritual, (3) clean-ingredient and zero-plastic trust cues.

**Visual Identity & Packaging:** Rich, tactile culinary world on the outer cardboard box (6 pouches inside; inner pouch is unprinted clear film). Custom iconography makes the 3-step prep unmistakable at a glance.

**Flavor System:** Master architecture for 1 Hero SKU that easily flexes across bone-broth and vegan/legume varieties.

## Guardrails: the work should not

- Lead with “edible packaging” or lab-tech language that makes the meal feel synthetic or experimental.
- Look like eco-austerity (beige craft paper clichés) or cheap college instant noodles.
- Overcomplicate the prep story. The ritual must read in three seconds.
- Wait on final recipes or exact box dimensions. Build on FPO dimensions and nutrition panels to hit Dec 18.
